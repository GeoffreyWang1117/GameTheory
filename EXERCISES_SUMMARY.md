# Game Theory Learning Platform - Exercise Summary

## 📊 Complete Exercise Catalog (34 exercises)

### Section 1: Introduction to Game Theory (3 exercises)
**Level**: Beginner
**Topics**: Fundamental concepts

1. **Prisoner's Dilemma** - Classic game illustrating conflict between individual and collective rationality
2. **Nash Equilibrium** - Finding stable strategy profiles where no player wants to deviate
3. **Dominant Strategies** - Identifying strategies that are always best regardless of opponent

### Section 2: Basic Concepts (4 exercises)
**Level**: Beginner-Intermediate
**Topics**: Game representations and solution concepts

4. **Zero-Sum Games** - Pure competition, minimax theorem, saddle points
5. **Mixed Strategies** - Randomization as optimal play, expected payoffs
6. **Payoff Matrices** - Matrix representations, best responses, IEDS
7. **Coordination Games** - Multiple equilibria, payoff vs risk dominance

### Section 3: Cooperative Games (3 exercises)
**Level**: Intermediate
**Topics**: Coalition formation and fair division

8. **Coalition Formation** - Characteristic functions, superadditivity, convexity
9. **Shapley Value** - Fair payoff distribution, marginal contributions, axioms
10. **The Core** - Stable allocations, coalitional rationality, balancedness

### Section 4: Repeated and Dynamic Games (4 exercises)
**Level**: Intermediate
**Topics**: Multi-stage interactions

11. **Iterated Prisoner's Dilemma** - Repeated play, strategies, tournaments
12. **Tit-for-Tat** - Famous strategies, forgiveness, retaliation
13. **Folk Theorem** - Sustaining cooperation, discount factors, punishment
14. **Sequential Games** - Backward induction, game trees, subgame perfection

### Section 5: Advanced Topics (5 exercises)
**Level**: Advanced
**Topics**: Cutting-edge applications

15. **Evolutionary Game Theory** - ESS, replicator dynamics, biological applications
16. **Hawk-Dove Game** - Conflict behavior, polymorphism, mixed ESS
17. **Asymmetric Information** - Bayesian games, beliefs, signaling
18. **Auction Theory** - Vickrey auctions, revenue equivalence, bidding strategies
19. **Mechanism Design** - VCG, incentive compatibility, voting rules

### Section 6: Bargaining and Negotiation (3 exercises)
**Level**: Advanced
**Topics**: Division of surplus

20. **Nash Bargaining Solution** - Axiomatic approach, fairness, IIA
21. **Rubinstein Bargaining** - Alternating offers, patience, first-mover advantage
22. **Ultimatum Game** - Behavioral economics, fairness preferences, experiments

### Section 7: Matching Theory and Market Design (3 exercises)
**Level**: Advanced
**Topics**: Two-sided markets (Nobel Prize 2012)

23. **Stable Marriage Problem** - Gale-Shapley algorithm, deferred acceptance, stability
24. **College Admissions** - Many-to-one matching, capacity constraints
25. **Kidney Exchange** - Real-world application, cycles, optimization

### Section 8: Voting and Social Choice (3 exercises)
**Level**: Advanced
**Topics**: Collective decision-making

26. **Voting Rules** - Plurality, Borda count, Condorcet methods
27. **Arrow's Impossibility Theorem** - No perfect voting system exists
28. **Strategic Voting** - Manipulation, truthfulness, Gibbard-Satterthwaite

### Section 9: Network Games (3 exercises)
**Level**: Advanced
**Topics**: Strategic interactions on networks

29. **Network Formation** - Link creation, stability, efficiency
30. **Diffusion and Cascades** - Information spread, adoption dynamics
31. **Congestion Games** - Traffic equilibrium, price of anarchy, Braess paradox

### Section 10: Correlated Equilibrium and Learning (3 exercises)
**Level**: Advanced
**Topics**: Beyond Nash equilibrium

32. **Correlated Equilibrium** - Public signals, coordination devices
33. **Regret Minimization** - No-regret learning, multiplicative weights
34. **Fictitious Play** - Belief-based learning, convergence

## 🏆 Key Theorems and Algorithms Covered

1. **Nash's Theorem** - Nash equilibrium exists in finite games
2. **Minimax Theorem** - Zero-sum games have value
3. **Folk Theorem** - Repeated games sustain cooperation
4. **Gale-Shapley Algorithm** - Stable matching always exists
5. **Arrow's Impossibility Theorem** - No perfect voting system
6. **Revenue Equivalence Theorem** - Auction formats yield same revenue
7. **Shapley's Uniqueness** - Unique solution to cooperative games
8. **VCG Mechanism** - Incentive compatible mechanism design
